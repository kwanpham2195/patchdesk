# 02 — Inbox scenario coverage and local interactions

**What to build:** A designer can review the inbox's important content, freshness, loading, failure, filtering, refresh, direct-entry, and pull-request-opening states through stable scenarios and local interactions.

**Blocked by:** 01 — Design app foundation and default inbox

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Empty, loading, request-failure, cached, and populated inbox scenarios are directly addressable.
- [ ] Queue filtering, search, sorting, refresh, saved-view, and selection behavior remain usable with mock data.
- [ ] Direct pull-request preview and opening a pull request use deterministic local responses.
- [ ] Running, waiting-for-author, checks-failing, updated-since-review, draft, and ready-to-merge rows are represented.
- [ ] Success and failure feedback is visible without changing real Patchdesk state.
- [ ] The scenarios remain faithful to the existing responsive inbox layout.
